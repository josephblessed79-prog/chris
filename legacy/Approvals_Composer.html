How this works

Read once. Two minutes.

The rule of the tool. Every name, quantity, price and vote is typed once, straight from the document in the file. The tool does the arithmetic, writes every amount in words, builds the comparison table, and produces the Approval, the Minute Sheet, the Checklist and the Verification Certificate. It invents nothing. Anything missing is shown as a red failure, not papered over. Nothing leaves this computer; no internet is used at any point.
The six steps

1 — Case Details. File reference, date, addressee, subject, and signatory.

2 — Items & Quotations. One card per item. Choose the entry mode per card: Quantity × unit cost for priced line items (shoes, cable, laptops), or Amount only where a supplier gives one overall figure (promotional items, general supplies). Under each item, one row per supplier — including those that did not quote. Tick Recommend on the winning row of each item; different items may go to different suppliers, and the documents will set the award out supplier by supplier, with each supplier's items and amount.

3 — Recommendation & Vote. The procurement method, the reasons for the recommendation, the vote, and (optionally) narrative composed from your notes.

4 — Folios & Attachments. The minute-sheet folio list and the letter's attachments list. One click seeds both from the supplier rows.

5 — Verification. Every control the supervisor would apply is run automatically. Green means the pack is internally consistent. Red tells you what to fix and, on that page, a Fix → button takes you straight to the field.

6 — Documents. Preview, print, or download each document as a Word file.

What the tool will not do

It will not read the paper quotations for you, and it will not confirm that a typed figure matches the folio — only a person with the file open in front of them can do that. The Verification Certificate therefore prints every figure with a folio box and a tick box, so the checker confirms each one against the source before the pack is carried for signature. Any document generated while checks are failing is stamped DRAFT — NOT CLEARED across the top.

Saving your work

Use Save Draft (bottom bar) at any time; a small .json file is downloaded. To continue later or on another computer, open this page and use Load Draft. Nothing is sent anywhere.
